# Product Vision

Build a coherent enterprise fintech platform that demonstrates the architecture and engineering principles used in serious financial infrastructure.

The platform spans:
- digital banking
- identity and authentication
- KYC/KYB
- consent
- accounts and wallets
- double-entry ledger
- payments and payment orchestration
- checkout and merchant infrastructure
- cards and digital payments
- A2A / instant-payment abstractions
- cross-border payments
- FX
- credit decisioning
- lending
- BNPL
- risk/fraud
- AML / transaction monitoring
- settlement and reconciliation
- accounting / general ledger
- regulatory/reporting abstractions
- audit and observability
- API and integration infrastructure

The goal is not to simulate every regulatory detail of one country. Build a jurisdiction-neutral core with adapters and policy/configuration boundaries for country-specific requirements.

Core quality attributes:
1. Financial correctness
2. Security
3. Auditability
4. Reconciliation
5. Resilience
6. Explainability
7. Operational transparency
8. Evolutionary scalability
