# Reconciliation Rules

- Preserve both internal and external evidence.
- Never silently discard unmatched records.
- Model reconciliation breaks explicitly.
- Matching must be deterministic and explainable.
- Resolution must be auditable.
- Tolerances and matching rules should be versioned/configurable where business significance requires it.
